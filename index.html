About

Dr. Zahir Ahmed Shaikh is an Assistant Professor in the School of Commerce and Management Studies at Sandip University, Nashik, where he has taught since November 2024. He teaches undergraduate and postgraduate courses in commerce and management, mentors students on projects and careers, and contributes to accreditation (NAAC/AQAR) and institutional development.

He has more than a decade of teaching experience. From 2011 to 2017 he taught BBA and BCA programmes at K. K. Wagh Arts, Commerce, Science and Computer Science College, Nashik, first as a Lecturer and then as a University of Pune–approved Assistant Professor. He then taught Machine Learning and Data Science fundamentals at The Standard School of Machine Learning in Mumbai.

His research spans human resource management, organizational behavior, leadership, healthcare management and workplace psychology. He focuses on burnout, stress, leadership styles, employee engagement, cyberloafing and corporate social responsibility. He earned his Ph.D. in Management from Lovely Professional University in 2025.
